Flagship Product

PocketMind Hybrid AI

Offline + Online AI

Private AI power, inside your pocket

PocketMind Hybrid AI is an Android AI assistant designed for people who want speed, privacy, and flexibility. It supports local offline LLMs, optional online models, custom GGUF loading, characters, document and image attachments, voice input, voice replies, image generation, model management, and Vulkan-ready mobile GPU acceleration in one polished mobile experience.

The goal is simple: give users a real AI workspace on their phone — not just a chatbot screen. Offline mode keeps prompts local on the device, while online mode gives access to fast cloud models when the user chooses them.

Public Privacy Policy

PocketMind Hybrid AI

Effective Date: May 26, 2026 · Developer: Muhammad Nouman Shakeel / PocketMind · Country: Pakistan

PocketMind Hybrid AI (“PocketMind,” “the App,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is an AI assistant app that allows users to chat with offline AI models, use optional online AI providers, attach documents and images, generate images, manage local models, and customize the app experience.

Summary: Offline features are processed locally on your device. Online features are optional and only send information to the selected third-party provider when you choose to use those features. PocketMind does not operate its own cloud server to collect, store, sell, or analyze your private conversations.

1. Information We Do Not Collect on Our Servers

PocketMind does not maintain developer-operated servers for collecting user conversations or files. We do not sell your personal data, use your conversations for advertising, collect your private chats on a PocketMind server, or collect your API keys on a PocketMind server.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

PocketMind may store information locally on your device, including chat messages, saved conversations, chat titles, custom characters, custom system prompts, app settings, API keys that you choose to save, downloaded offline AI model files, imported GGUF model files, image generation model files, temporary cache files, extracted text from attached files, generated images if you choose to save them, and backup files if you export them.

3. API Keys

PocketMind allows you to enter API keys for supported online AI providers. These keys are stored locally on your device. When you use an online provider, the relevant key is sent from your device directly to that provider so the request can be processed. You are responsible for keeping your API keys private and following each provider’s terms, billing rules, usage limits, and privacy policies.

4. Offline AI Processing

When using offline GGUF models or other local model features, PocketMind processes AI requests on your device. In offline mode, prompts and responses are not sent to online AI providers by PocketMind. Offline performance depends on your device hardware, available RAM, CPU, Android Vulkan/mobile GPU support where available, storage, and selected model size.

5. Online AI Processing

If you select an online model or online image generation provider, PocketMind may send request data directly to the selected provider. Depending on the feature, this data may include your prompt, recent chat context, system or character instructions, extracted document text, encoded image data, image generation prompts, generation settings, and provider authentication tokens.

PocketMind does not control how third-party providers process, store, retain, or use data after it is sent to them. Your use of these providers is subject to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

6. Document and File Attachments

PocketMind can read or extract content from files you attach, such as PDF, DOCX, TXT, code, CSV, JSON, log files, and images. If you use an offline model, extracted text remains local to the device. If you use an online model, extracted text may be sent to the selected online provider as part of the request.

7. Image Attachments and Vision Features

PocketMind may allow image attachments for vision-capable online models. When an image is attached and a supported online vision provider is selected, the image may be encoded and sent directly to that provider for analysis. Offline text models may not understand image pixels directly.

8. Image Generation

PocketMind supports image generation through offline and online modes. Offline image generation processes prompts and models locally when a supported local image model is used. Free or premium online image generation providers may receive your prompt and generation settings when you choose those modes.

9. Backups

PocketMind may allow you to export backup files. These backups may include saved chats, prompts, characters, and settings. You are responsible for storing backup files securely.

10. Permissions

  • File/document access when you choose to import or attach files.
  • Notification permission for downloads or response notifications.
  • Storage/gallery access when saving generated images, depending on Android version.
  • Internet access for online AI providers, model downloads, online image generation, and API key validation.

11. Third-Party Services

PocketMind may connect to third-party services when you use online features, including Groq, Google Gemini / Google AI services, Hugging Face, Together AI, Pollinations, or model hosting platforms. These services are independent third parties and their handling of data is governed by their own policies.

12. Data Sharing

PocketMind does not sell your personal data. Data may be shared only at your direction when you choose an online model or provider, for model downloads, for legal compliance, or for security and abuse prevention if necessary.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally in PocketMind remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage, uninstall the App, delete chats, remove models, clear cache, remove API keys, delete generated images, or delete exported backups. Data sent to third-party providers is subject to those providers’ retention policies.

14. Security

PocketMind aims to handle user data securely and limit data access to what is needed for the selected feature. However, no app, device, network, or storage system is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, lock screen, backups, exported files, API keys, and sensitive files.

15. Children’s Privacy

PocketMind is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use AI services or online accounts without parental consent.

16. Medical, Legal, Financial, and Professional Disclaimer

PocketMind is for informational and educational purposes only. It does not provide professional medical, legal, financial, investment, or other licensed professional services. Always verify important information independently and consult qualified professionals where appropriate.

17. AI Output Accuracy

AI-generated responses may be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, offensive, unsafe, or misleading. You are responsible for reviewing and verifying AI-generated content before relying on it.

18. International Users

If you use PocketMind outside Pakistan, your information may be processed by third-party providers in other countries when you use online features.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features, provider changes, legal requirements, or security improvements. The updated version will be posted at this same public privacy policy URL with an updated effective date.

20. Contact
