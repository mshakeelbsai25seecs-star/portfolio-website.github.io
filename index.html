PocketMind Hybrid AI

Effective Date: May 26, 2026 · Developer: Muhammad Nouman Shakeel / PocketMind · Country: Pakistan

PocketMind Hybrid AI is an AI assistant app that allows users to chat with offline AI models, use optional online AI providers, attach documents and images, generate images, manage local models, and customize the app experience.

Summary: Offline features are processed locally on your device. Online features are optional and only send information to the selected third-party provider when you choose to use those features.

1. Information Stored Locally

PocketMind may store chat messages, saved conversations, custom characters, app settings, API keys, downloaded offline AI model files, imported GGUF model files, temporary cache files, extracted attachment text, generated images, and backup files locally on your device.

2. Online AI Processing

If you select an online model or image generation provider, PocketMind may send request data directly to the selected provider. PocketMind does not control third-party retention or processing after the data is sent.

3. Files, Images, and Backups

Attached documents, images, generated images, and backups are controlled by the user. If used with an online provider, relevant data may be sent to that provider for processing.

4. Permissions

  • File/document access when you import or attach files.
  • Notification permission for downloads or response notifications.
  • Internet access for online AI providers, downloads, and API key validation.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or support, contact the developer at: +92 340 5055603.

Public Privacy Policy

BusinessLedger AI

Effective Date: May 31, 2026 · Developer: Muhammad Nouman Shakeel / BusinessLedger AI · Country: Pakistan

BusinessLedger AI is a business ledger and AI assistant app for small businesses, shopkeepers, wholesalers, distributors, tailors, pharmacies, mobile shops, and service businesses.

Summary: BusinessLedger AI stores business records locally on your device. Online AI features are optional and are used only when you ask the app to parse or process text with an online provider. AI output is never saved directly without your confirmation.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App may store customers, suppliers, phone numbers, notes, ledger entries, sales, payments, product and stock data, reminder records, business profile details, receipt settings, invoice numbering, app settings, onboarding state, generated receipt text, and temporary PDF files locally on your device.

2. Online AI Processing

When you use AI parsing, AI chat, voice-to-khata text processing, or WhatsApp shared-message parsing, selected text and relevant business context may be sent from your device to the configured online AI provider so it can return a structured result.

3. Confirmation Before Saving

AI-generated khata drafts require user review and confirmation before saving. You should verify customer names, amounts, paid values, remaining balances, notes, products, and other details before committing them.

4. API Keys and AI Provider Access

If you save an online AI API key, the App stores it locally and uses it to send requests directly to the selected provider. The key is protected with Android Keystore-backed encryption where supported.

5. Receipts, PDFs, and Sharing

Editable receipt text and printable PDF receipts are shared only when you choose to share them through Android’s share sheet, WhatsApp, email, file apps, or other apps installed on your device.

6. Notifications and Reminders

The App may schedule local payment reminder notifications when you choose to create them. Notifications are generated on your device and may include customer names, balances, or reminder text.

7. Permissions

  • Internet access is used for online AI processing.
  • Network state access may be used to detect connectivity.
  • Notification permission may be requested for scheduled reminders.
  • File sharing is used for receipt/PDF export through Android’s secure sharing system.
  • Speech recognition may be used when you dictate text.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your business data. Data may be shared only when you choose an online AI feature, share a receipt/PDF/reminder, contact support, comply with legal requirements, or use future cloud backup/sync features if added later.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete data by using the App’s clear local data option, deleting entries manually where supported, removing the AI key, clearing Android app storage, or uninstalling the App.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, support, or deletion requests, contact the developer at: +92 340 5055603.
